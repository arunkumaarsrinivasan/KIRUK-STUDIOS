# Workflow — orchestrator

Arun orchestrates. The agent files documents. Making still happens in the world (paper, code, call).

This is the **project** loop. The 17-day [spine.md](./spine.md) is the **brand-thinking** loop. A project may run while the spine is only half-filled. Do not wait for day 17 to giggle.

## Five steps

```
1 Idea generation     spark in the workbook / this KB
2 Project definition  brief + theme + experimental approach
3 Design & development iterate, assets, prototypes
4 Review & feedback   client (or public) in the loop
5 Delivery            finish + write outcomes back into the workbook / KB
```

### 1 — Idea generation

Capture the spark. Source: printed workbook, spoken dump, survey, X, a scribble. File it (`chapters/`, `ideas.md`). Do not decorate yet.

### 2 — Project definition

Write, short:

- Who is the one client (or “kiruk / community”)?
- Which **theme** (one primary)?
- What is experimental about the approach (what we do not know yet)?
- What we will not do (trend reskin, committee, start-rebuild).

This is the kiruk version of a brief. It can live as a stub in `projects.md` until a universe/ISM/product folder is allowed to exist.

### 3 — Design and development

Block by block ([constraints.md](./constraints.md)). Paper first when it is a universe/ISM/product. Prototype. Do not skip to polish.

### 4 — Review and feedback

One-to-one review with the client, or a public loop if the project is OSS / survey / giggle-in-public. Fold feedback in. Show the change (see [share-loop.md](./share-loop.md)).

### 5 — Delivery

Ship the thing. Then **document the outcome** back into this folder (what we learned, what broke, what to keep). The workbook stays a living spark, not a trophy.

## Relation to studio OS

Portal lifecycle, OpenSpec, templates — those are for **durable / paid universes**. This five-step is the thinking for experiments _and_ for how a one-to-one job should feel. When Face 1 resumes, this loop should inform it, not the other way around.
