# 06 — The People

**Thinking job:** One person, not everyone. Snapshot used for every later sentence.

**State:** complete enough 2026-09-02. Persona **Sanxia** named. Feeds [people.md](../people.md) and `positioning.md`.

Sanxia is a **thinking persona**, not a live client. No universe folder. No fees.

---

## Who (the one, not the market)

Not the person who wants the basic stuff, copies the market, and stops thinking.

The one: a **vision** they will take through the best of the best. Lifestyle, school, creative vision — they do not care which container. They want the **impossible**. They need help on strategy, the idea, and the creative path. Passion first, not profit. They work the idea day and night.

kiruk’s job: walk that path and make it a **proper whole**.

**Face of the one:** **Sanxia**, 29, she. Illustrator. Full portrait in [people.md](../people.md).

---

## 1. What they wish someone to help with

They already have an idea. They want it **better**.

Help that shows up (multidisciplinary — this is what Kiruk Studio holds):

- **Brand strategy**
- Website, **MVP**, app design — fill the MVP, hands-on **UI spec**, and whatever else the thing needs
- A **system and process** (so it holds)
- **Not** direct marketing. A **creative / marketing website**. A creative mark. **Creative direction** for the whole branding, plus the **ideology** they already have
- **Creative tools** (kiruk may also be making those)

---

## 2. Long-term desire

They often **do not know** the end they want. They have an idea they want to take **national or international** — a benchmark from the root, plus everything around it.

They know _what_. They do not know _how_. They cannot figure the path. They cannot make a **whole** they can sustain, stay consistent, and keep building **even if kiruk is not in the room**.

They want to express the creative and innovative way through the **Kiruk Framework** (seed). Not only that wrapper. Not money as the point. **Passion.**

Reject: follow what others do, do what the market does.

Wanted: someone on the strategy + idea + creative path who will take it to proper.

---

## 3. Biggest worries

- **Wasting money**
- After they pour it, it still looks **unprofessional**
- **Standing out:** they did their part (product, structure). It still does not stand out. Design and design decisions are not the best they could be, next to others.

## 4. How they buy

Slow. Lots of research. They are about to pour money.

Emotional first: _will this person sit with us or not?_ Logic exists, but money constraint often wins over clean logic.

They go with **recommendations** — what other people suggest. That is what is happening. Founder wants to **break** that pattern. Selling (14) has to hold both facts.

## 11. Sanxia (persona, not a live job)

**Sanxia**, 29, she. Digital illustration + branding already. Wants a gallery business she can sell online.

**Help:** a unique **3D** website that _is_ the art gallery — see the work in space, feel it, tell the biggest story clearly. She is too good at illustration; she needs someone to represent it at the best of the best.

**Frustration:** idea, no direction. Cannot translate imagination into the product. Arts major, not tech. **Tech frustration.**

**External:** continuous illustration work → limited time. Does not know the tech.

**Tried:** Framer, Figma, random plot/prompt, unknown stack. Failed. End product is not the vision. Lost _(spoken: in the bale)_. Looks unfinished.

**What kiruk would change (every project, not only hers):** take it as a challenge. Extraordinary delivery — storytelling, essence, emotion they want to convey. Awards-level craft as the _bar_, not a clone of an awards homepage, not a scraped category list.
