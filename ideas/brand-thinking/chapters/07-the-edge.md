## The three (working)

1. **Express, don’t coat.** Web, mobile, anything — do not settle. Go to the edge others hesitate on (hassle is not the filter). Choose what brings out the _best_ of the brand and the idea. Show how the brand **should be**, not a coating that only looks different.
2. **Kiruk Framework.** Bring the idea out like a child: unlock their creativity, understand it clearly, show a **variety** of frames, make it easier, bring it to **life** as unique storytelling. That is the difference in the same field. Still needs a proper foundation so a serious client can board it.
3. **A pack, not “just a studio.”** In-house products / ISM / creative tools **and** services that unleash the idea to the best of the best. Idea → a product. Not a services-only shop. Not a product-only lab.

## 1. Different from other creative studios

Do not settle for less. Try something **new, to the edge**, and achieve it. Try what people hesitate to try because it is a hassle — hassle is not the thought. The thought is: what suits, what brings out the best.

Unique design that **shows** the brand and the idea. Express how the brand should be. Not coating it. Not differentiating for the sake of looking other.

Kiruk Framework: their best idea, unique, **storytelling**. That is how kiruk is different in the same field.

## 2. What competitors would say you are bad at (honest)

- Unique work can take **more time**. Stuck in the middle is hard to push forward.
- The client may not understand _why_ it is happening. kiruk’s task is still to do it better **and** take it faster — delay can still happen.
- **More iterations** than usual.
- **Changes design frequently** — people may find it hard to trust.
- Can go **out of context**, take things the difficult / different way.
- Needs the **edge end-to-end**. Needs to be **specific**, not vague, when presenting.
- Feels mature and still wants more **industry depth**. Needs ideas **per project**.
- Still improving. Does not treat this list as a forever identity.

## 3. What competitors would say you are good at

Telling the story. Bringing the idea out. Idea → concept, easily. Fun, **gamification**, new ideas. **Interlink** many things and make something from them. Mingle with people, grow their idea, take their creativity to the max. Taste — people love how it is designed.
