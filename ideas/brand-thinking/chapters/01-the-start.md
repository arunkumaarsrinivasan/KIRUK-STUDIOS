# 01 — The Start (dump)

Spoken fill of printed pages 19–21. 2026-09-02.  
Routed to: association, why-studio, inspirations, constraints, ideas, tensions, positioning.

## Q1 — Known for

Simple yet creative. Expressive. Not a trend follower — a trend setter. Will not take trend work. Not “a design to be created”; it should revolutionize. They recognize it as kiruk, understand it, then want it.

Seek: curious, design-like people.

## Q2 — Instant association

Pen, paper, scribbling → kiruk.

AI age: people skip their own thinking. Bring back sketching. Set a **Kiruk Framework** so someone with an idea can unleash it (same _job_ as this workbook, aimed at the client). Take it to the extreme. Rebellion + curiosity, not ordinary. Warm hug they can lean on. Notebook culture.

Not: get client, then execute. **Bring out the best of the client first**, then work.

## Q3 — Keep doing

Writing, scribbling, sketching (5–6 years). Want the studio. For mad people. Tamil: kiruk = madness + scribble. Bring out that madness. Work with creative craft people. Paper → digital.

## Q4 — Learn / unlearn

Learn: show a project as **storytelling**, then to people who want it made (product / digital). Fix lagged **foundation** — what to make, how. Trust that extraordinary is possible; they stay curious enough for best-of-best.

Unlearn (implied): start without a spine; rebuild forever; speed without core.

## Q5 — Successful picture

Not money-first. Trust stays. Not a studio that pitches the market and performs “greatest of all time.”

Impossible things; they may lack the urge or the idea. Work until it works. Bring out curiosity and potential that is not in the world yet. Showcase. Then: _I can trust this._

Huge success: pen / paper / scribble resonates as kiruk, nothing else.

## Q6 — Why a brand

Cannot stay independent artist / creative developer. Studio, not a personal site. Open and transparent: how, why, gain and loss. Self-expression on any surface (animation, weird, plugin). Against doing everything fast. Scribble → core → iterate. From India / Arun: you have creativity; you are not expressing it. Cracked is not enough until it is shown.

## Q7 — Two inspirations

1. Immersive Garden — creative studio, ultimate work.
2. Nothing — from-scratch product, ideology + tech.

Intersection: product **and** creative development. Feeling, not only “does it work.” Simple, revolutionary, never imagined — that’s how it should have been.

## Holding back (with Q7)

Linking creativity to technology. Feels beginner. Structure slips. Distraction. Spin-out. Projects that never end (client risk). Working on foundation, showcase, how the studio holds.

## Q8 — Movie scream

Stop stalling at the start. Don’t hold back. Implement. Foundation, block by block, possible end, steps, recover when it breaks. Do not make suddenly and rebuild forever.
