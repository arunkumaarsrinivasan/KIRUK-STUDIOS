## 1. What you do (product and service)

Most studios pick **product or service**. kiruk plans **both**.

**Service:** one-to-one experimental making with a curious mind (site, creative work). Client example in chat only.

**Lab / in-house:** Kirukism products, Kirukargals (word still overlaps collaborator vs line — unset), Nomadism, Heroism. Names only until scribble + spawn.

**Also:** open-source design tool for work that does not exist yet. Surveys. Design collaborator.

**Strong words (product / service):**

- Creative website
- Mobile app
- Web app
- Assets
- Creative tools
- Plugins
- Resources
- Digital product
- Later, maybe: a **physical product** that connects technology and creativity

## 2. How people should feel when they hear the name

Absurd on purpose: enthusiasm · craziness · crackpot · excitement · intrigue · joy. Then **excited and curious** at the work. No fixed form in art and design.

## 3. Brand style

Scribbled. Black and white, plus white+colour and black+colour. Absurd, artistic, playful, whimsical. Eye-strong. Many elements, still cohesive. Logo after fine-tune.
