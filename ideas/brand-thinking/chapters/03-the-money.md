## 1. First month — start small, stay curious

Not a flagship. Not a big project as the opening move.

The first month should be **small enough to begin** and **curious enough** that more people (and later, more of the right clients) want in.

Work that could do that job (examples, not a menu):

- an MVP
- a branding site
- brand strategy
- GTM (go-to-market)
- a redesign
- a web design made personal — something that puts kiruk **around people**

**Capacity:** at most **three** small projects, **or** one more substantial single project. The “substantial” threshold is private. Do not put it in git.

The point of the first money is not scale. It is a start that gathers the next rooms.

---

## 2. What the business should feel like (how money is earned)

Money is real when the **right people** approach: curious minds, crack people, someone with an **innovative project or a technology** who needs a design / creative partner.

They should **trust**, **realize**, and **approach**. Not judge. Not interrogate the craft to death.

**Doctor-trust:** like a doctor. You do not walk in to judge the doctor. You hear what you need. You can have a better conversation. You do not sit there asking “why is it like this, why is it like that” from a place of not getting it.

The real business:

- people approach
- they understand the process
- that makes the work easier
- kiruk does what needs doing from this side — then **more than that**
- the job is still to **make the best out of the client**
- but the client comes in already trusting

That is how kiruk wants to get paid. Trust first. Work that earns it. Not a client who never gets it.

Conversation is wanted. Judgment and endless why-why from people who do not get the process is not.
