# Why a studio — not a personal site

Filed from The Start (01), Q6.

## The move

Cannot remain an independent artist / independent creative developer who “made a thing.”

This is **kiruk the studio**, not a personal URL. Not a digital presence for Arun’s name. A studio from India / from Arun Kumaar that tells people: you already have creativity; you are not expressing it.

Being cracked, curious, or crazy is **not** the showcase. Showing the work in the world is.

## What the studio must be able to hold

- **Open enough:** people can see how it is made, why, and what they gain or lose. (Public process; money and client internals stay closed per existing studio rules.)
- **Any surface:** animation, weird digital, plugin, product — not one digital product forever.
- **Against speed-without-core:** people run everything fast. kiruk takes the scribble, finds the core, iterates.

## Both paths (Name 02)

Studios usually choose product **or** service. kiruk runs **both**: experimental one-to-one making, and in-house / OSS products. Surfaces: creative website, web app, mobile, assets, creative tools, plugins, resources, digital product — and service as site or creative work for a curious mind. Later, maybe a physical thing that joins technology and creativity.

**Creative** is not optional copy. Strip it and the soul goes. The business word is **studio**, not company.

Edge (07): not _only_ a creative studio. A **pack** — in-house products / tools / ISM **and** services that unleash the idea. Express the brand as it should be. Don’t coat it.

## How money arrives (Money 03)

The studio is real when the right people **approach** — curious, crack, an innovative project or tech that needs a creative partner — and treat kiruk with **doctor-trust**. First month is small and curious so more of those people can gather. Not a company chasing a flagship invoice. Figures stay off-git.

## Studio before company (Setup 05)

**Kiruk Studio** is the name and the practice. A registered company waits until a private yearly-income threshold says it is needed. Tax IDs wait with that. Pipes that already exist (`kiruk.in`, kirukism on IG/YouTube, a separate money account, a Bangalore scribble desk) are enough to start. Studio email and a dedicated business bank come when the rest is standing.

This is still **not** a personal URL. It is a studio that has not incorporated yet — not an independent-artist site.

## Known for (Q1, as thinking)

Simple yet creative. Expressive. Trend **setter**, not follower. Will not take work that wants a trend reskin. Not “a design to be created” — work that **revolutionizes**, that they recognize as kiruk, then understand, then want.

Who: **curious, design-like people.**
