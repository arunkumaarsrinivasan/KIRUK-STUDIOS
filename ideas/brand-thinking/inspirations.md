# Inspirations — poles, not templates

Filed from The Start (01), Q7. Use as **thinking poles**. Do not clone either.

## Pole A — Immersive Garden

A creative studio. The work is the proof. “Ultimate” as in: the thing itself is the argument.

**Steal the thinking:** craft and feeling can be the product. People should see something they have not seen.

**Do not steal:** their look, their clients, their pipeline.

## Pole B — Nothing

A product company that starts from an ideology (what _nothing_ is), then tech, then the object. From scratch. A team whose thinking is visible in the thing. Sets a benchmark others copy; the real thinking stays with the people who made it.

**Steal the thinking:** idea + technology together. Creative is not decoration on a default product. Out of the box because the **from-scratch ideology** is real.

**Do not steal:** their hardware, their marketing kit, “be the Indian Nothing.”

## The intersection (kiruk’s bet)

**Product and creative development.** People see something beautiful that was not in the world.

In an AI stretch, many only ask “does it work?” This pass says: **feeling and impression** matter, without fake hype. Show what the brand is. Show how the thing works, expressively.

**Simple. Revolutionary. They never imagined it — and that’s how it should have been.**

## How this feeds pitch later

Lead with the intersection, not with “we are a studio like X.” Proof will be work, not name-drop. Name-drop is private reference until a case exists.

Public process (X-shaped loops, OSS tools) is documented in [share-loop.md](./share-loop.md). Steal the loop, not the hustle kit.
