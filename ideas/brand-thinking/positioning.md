## Hypotheses from The Start through Edge (07)

- **Place:** the intersection of a product that starts from ideology + tech, and a studio that makes work people have not seen. Not “an agency.” Not “a personal creative.” **Both** product and service, on purpose. A **pack**, not _only_ a creative studio.
- **For:** **Sanxia** (persona, 29, illustrator) — and people like her. A passionate maker with a vision they cannot yet execute or say. They want the impossible and a whole that stands out. **Not** the person who copies the market. Lab work uses the same intimacy. Also designers who need a tool that does not exist yet.
- **Why us (Edge 07, three):** (1) express the brand as it should be — don’t coat it, don’t settle, go to the edge others hesitate on; (2) **Kiruk Framework** — childlike unlock, variety of frames, storytelling uniqueness, bring the idea to life; (3) pack of in-house products/tools/ISM **and** services that unleash the idea.
- **Honest cost:** unique work can take longer, more iterations, design may change; must stay specific, explain the stuck, earn trust.
- **Hire for:** brand strategy, site/MVP/app + UI spec, system/process, creative marketing site (not direct ads), creative direction + their ideology, sometimes creative tools. Sanxia: a **3D** gallery site you can feel, that tells the biggest story.
- **Feel of the name:** absurd, crackpot, enthusiasm, intrigue, joy — then curiosity at the work.
- **Speak to them (not Personality lock):** down to earth, relatable, honest, warm, expert, confident. They value creativity, clear direction, aesthetics, freedom.
- **Look (not locked):** scribble, B&W plus colour on white or black, playful/whimsical/absurd, eye-strong, many parts still one. They notice **artistic or indie**, colourful, dark moody high-contrast, dreamy, bold — Visuals (12) still unset.
- **Against:** trend-reskins, AI-speed with no thinking, studios that pitch the market back to the market, committee delivery, product-only or service-only as a rule, direct marketing as the offer, unfinished Framer/Figma DIY that is not the vision, coating a brand to look “different.”
- **Bar (every project):** extraordinary — storytelling, essence, emotion. Awards-level craft as a challenge, not a clone.
- **Hangouts:** Instagram, blogs, sometimes Substack. Not TikTok. LinkedIn not the main room.
- **Lenses:** criticism, nepotism, absurdism, naturalism, heroism — picked per project, not as a slogan stack.
- **Naming family:** metaphorical -isms around _kiruk_ (Kirukism, Nomadism, Heroism, Kirukal, Invitism, Polymnism). New names are seeds, not spawned ISMs. Studio name **kiruk** kept.
- **Proof later:** work that felt impossible, shown as story; pen/paper in the culture; trust; giggle and OSS as public proof.
- **How money arrives (Money 03):** start small, curious enough to gather the next people. Right client approaches (curious, crack, innovative project or tech). **Doctor-trust** — they do not judge the craft; they converse. First month is not a flagship. Figures stay off-git. Today they often buy via **recommendations**; founder wants to break that.
- **How real it is (Setup 05):** Kiruk Studio in practice before a registered company. Pipes that exist are enough. URL unset between subdomain and path.

## Pitch discipline (when we get there)

- Pitch the **thinking**, then the work. Not “we do branding.”
- Do not lead with the OS, tokens, or portal.
- Do not lead with money. Trust is the success picture. Doctor-trust is how the business feels when it is real.
- One sentence they can repeat. Written after bones, not before.
