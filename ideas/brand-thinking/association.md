# Association — what should lock without a pitch

Filed from The Start (01). Refine in Visuals (12) and Story (11).

## Look (Name 02 — not locked)

Scribble. Black/white plus colour on white and colour on black. Absurd, playful, whimsical, eye-strong. Many parts, one piece. Logo incoming after fine-tune.

## Instant lock

**Pen. Paper. Scribbling. → kiruk.**

Not “a cool studio.” Not a trend look. If they see a notebook or a scribble, the name that should arrive is kiruk.

## Why this lock (thinking)

A lot of current making skips the person’s own thinking. kiruk’s bet is that the **old way** (sketch, scribble, then a structure) is the missing piece — and that it can be taught / triggered, not only performed by the founder.

## Kiruk Framework (seed)

A workbook-like **trigger** whose job is the same _class_ as the 17 Day book (it helped Arun voice a brand), aimed at the **client’s** idea:

- they already have something
- bring it out **like a child** (unlock, not perform for them)
- understand it clearly
- show a **variety of frames** so they can choose
- take it toward the extreme (curiosity, rebellion, not ordinary)
- bring it to **life** as unique storytelling

Tone: **warm hug** they can lean on. Culture of notebooks.

This is not “we received a brief.” **Bring out the best of them first**, then make. **Express the brand as it should be — don’t coat it.**

People (06): they want the world to see **direction and creativity as one thing**, and cannot express it. That is the **Kiruk Framework**’s job-to-be — still a seed, form unset. Sanxia is the face: illustration that cannot yet become the 3D gallery she can feel.

Edge (07): this Framework is the unique part. It still needs a **foundation and structure** before a serious client boards it. Do not spawn a product from that sentence.

## What we keep doing so it sticks

Write. Scribble. Sketch (years already). Stay a studio for **mad people** (_kiruk_ = madness + scribble). Work with craft people. Carry paper into digital without killing the kirukal.

Desk (Setup 05): Bangalore, not fancy. Graphic tablet is the scribble surface beside the laptop. The lock does not wait on a prettier room.

## Success test

Huge success is not a month of invoices. It is: pen / paper / scribble resonates as kiruk and **not** as anyone else.
