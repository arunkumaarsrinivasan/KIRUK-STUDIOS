# People — who sits in the room

Filed from People (06). One person, not everyone. Use this snapshot before writing pitch.

**Persona name:** Sanxia. She / 29. A thinking face for “the one,” not a live client, not a `kiruk-projects/` universe, no spawn.

---

## Sanxia (the one, made specific)

She is too good at **illustration**. She already has digital illustration and branding. She wants a **business illustration / gallery** she can sell online.

What she needs help with: a **3D website** that _is_ the gallery — you can see her work in space and **feel** it. A unique site that shows the illustration clearly and tells her **biggest story**. She needs someone to represent that and take it the best of the best way.

**Frustration:** an idea, no direction. Cannot translate imagination into the product she wants. **Tech frustration** — arts, not tech.

**External blockers:** she has continuous illustration work, so **limited time**. She does not know the tech. She majored in arts, not tech.

**Tried:** Framer, Figma, a random plot/prompt, some unknown stack. Failed. The end never looks like what she envisioned. She got lost _(spoken: “in the bale”)_; whatever she tried looks **unfinished**.

**Escape:** an end product that is not the vision.

**Before kiruk (Edge 07):** off track, distracted, referenced someone else’s look, went sloppy, out of context from what they actually had in mind.

kiruk’s bar for her (and for **each** project taken): extraordinary — storytelling, essence, emotion she wants to convey. Awards-level craft as a _challenge_, not a clone of an awards site, not a category list. **Express, don’t coat.**

---

## The pattern (not only Sanxia)

A passionate maker with an idea they work day and night. Vision first — they want the **impossible**, a whole that **stands out** and still runs if kiruk leaves the room. Not profit as the point. Not “do what the market does.”

They know _what_. They do not know _how_. They cannot say it. The site / content / marketing do not show it yet.

They want to express that path through the **Kiruk Framework** (seed). Not only the wrapper. Passion.

## What they hire kiruk for

Brand strategy (idea → better). Website / MVP / app, UI spec, the rest of the fill. System and process. **Creative** marketing site — not direct marketing. Creative mark. **Creative direction** + their ideology. Sometimes creative tools. For Sanxia: 3D gallery site as the product. Multidisciplinary, held by Kiruk Studio.

## Worries

Wasting money. Paying and still looking unprofessional. Structured product that still does not stand out because design decisions are weak next to others.

## How they buy (today)

Slow research. Emotional: _will this person sit with us?_ Recommendations from other people. Founder wants to break that. Doctor-trust is the _desired_ arrival; recommendation is the _current_ arrival.

## Hangouts

Instagram. Blogs (DIY). Sometimes Substack. LinkedIn is techy, not the main room. **Not TikTok.**

## Blockers

Perfectionism. Fear of the wrong choice. Overthinking. Missing skill. **Tech frustration.** No strategy / direction. No brand consistency.

Already tried (pattern): DIY → cheapest freelancer → still alone → random marketing with no plan. Sanxia’s DIY was Framer / Figma / prompts / unknown stack.

## After the work

Seen and understood. Ready to launch. Clear and professionally set. Same vibe, same taste.

## How to talk to them (hypothesis, not Personality lock)

Down to earth, relatable, honest, warm, expert, confident.

They value: creativity, clear direction, aesthetics, freedom.

Taste they notice: **artistic or indie** · playful and colourful · dark, moody, high contrast · whimsical or dreamy · bold and expressive. Visuals (12) still unset.

## Stage

Stuck · changing direction · beginner. Sick of DIY. Rebranding or embarrassed by results. Grew the brand themselves. Finally ready to invest. Want the world to see direction + creativity as one thing, and cannot express it.
