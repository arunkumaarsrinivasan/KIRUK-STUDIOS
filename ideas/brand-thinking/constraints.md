# Constraints — what to stop, what to learn

Filed from The Start (01), Q4 + holding back + Q8.

## Movie scream (the one stop)

**Stop stalling at the start.** Creativity is there. The extreme is not being pushed. Implementation is held back.

Needed instead:

- a foundation
- **block by block**
- a sense of how the end might look
- a step for each move
- a way to tackle when it does not work as planned

Forbidden pattern: make something suddenly, then rebuild again and again.

If this were a film: **start implementing. Don’t hold back.**

This knowledge base _is_ that foundation for brand strategy. It is not an excuse to pause making forever — it is the spine so making doesn’t spin.

## Learn

When a project arrives, **show it as storytelling**, then to people who want it made (product / digital). Trust that kiruk can make extraordinary work comes from that, plus a structure that knows what to make and how.

## Filter (Money 03)

The default client kiruk does **not** want: someone who never gets the process and only interrogates (“why this, why that”) without trust.

Wanted: people who approach, understand the process, converse. Doctor-trust. Still bring out their best — conversation is not forbidden; judgment of the craft is.

**Not the client (People 06):** the person who wants the basic stuff, copies the market, and stops thinking. **The one (face: Sanxia):** vision, passion, impossible, a whole they can sustain. Arts not tech. Idea with no direction. DIY (Framer/Figma/prompts) that looks unfinished. They worry about wasting money and looking unprofessional after they pay. They buy slow, via recommendations — founder wants to break that, not pretend it is not happening.

**Bar:** each taken project is a challenge to deliver extraordinary work — storytelling, essence, emotion. Not a cheap reskin. Not a clone of an awards site. **Express, don’t coat.**

## Cost of the edge (Edge 07)

Unique work can take longer and need more iterations. That is not the same as start-rebuild _from zero_ — but frequent design change without explaining _why_ will break doctor-trust. Be **specific**, not vague. Show the situation when stuck. Go to the edge end-to-end.

Do not treat “I still need industry depth” as a reason not to file the next block (same as “I am still a beginner”).

First-month money: small, curious, at most three small projects or one more substantial one (threshold private). Not a flagship as the opening move.

Do not wait for a registered company, tax IDs, a fancy office, or a studio email to take the first right client. Incorporation is a later block (Setup 05).

## Unlearn

- Execute-the-brief as the opening move
- Trend as the brief
- Speed that skips the scribble
- Start-rebuild as a workflow
- “I am still a beginner” as a reason not to file the next block

## Holding back (honest)

Still linking creativity to technology. Structure slips. Distraction. Things spin; hard to return to position. Going outside the box is fine; **a project that never ends** is the client-facing risk.

Work on: foundation, how to showcase, how the studio holds.

## Founder energy (keep)

Keep writing and scribbling. The studio is for mad people. That practice is the association engine — see [association.md](./association.md).
