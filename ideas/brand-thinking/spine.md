# Spine — 17 days as thinking operations

Source of the _sequence_: Boring Studios _17 Day Brand_ workbook (personal use).  
This file is kiruk’s **operations map**, not a reproduction of the book.

Rule: study the spine, write kiruk’s version. Same as client-flow research.

## Why a spine exists

Brand strategy fails when visuals come first, or when every thought lives in one founder’s head. The spine forces **order**: dump → name → survival → energy → legal realness → who it’s for → why you → purpose/mission/vision/values → outside world → personality → story → pictures last → how people hire you → how you run a job → weekly repetition.

kiruk already has a lot of the _outputs_ (vision doc, templates, portal). This pass is for the **thinking underneath**, so pitch and position later come from a filled spine, not from the old draft.

## What kiruk steals vs rejects (thinking)

**Steal:** backwards dump before decoration; one audience; unique edge as few true things; bones before visuals; one hire-path; deposit/contract before make; offboard as part of trust.

**Reject:** becoming their template studio; 20-question mills as voice; putting money in git; skipping the kirukal; treating this PDF as the kiruk OS.

## How this improves the studio

Each filled operation becomes a **sentence we can pitch from**. Until then, public kiruk copy stays the old draft, and this folder holds the better thinking.
