# One-to-one — designer and client

Filed from the 2026-09-02 overview. Complements [why-studio.md](./why-studio.md).

## The dynamic

kiruk is a **creative studio where one designer works directly with one client on a project.**

That is the unit of work. Documentation should read like that: two people, one experiment, not an agency org chart.

## What this means for thinking

- **Intimacy:** bring out the client’s best first (The Start). A committee cannot sit in that room.
- **Experimental:** the project is allowed to be a test of a theme, not a template delivery.
- **Capacity:** the live repo still says max 2–3 client universes. One-to-one is the _relationship shape_, not “only one universe ever.” Tension logged in [tensions.md](./tensions.md).
- **Lab work:** self-initiated pieces (giggle, OSS, surveys) still have a “client” — sometimes that client is kiruk itself, or a community. Same five-step workflow.

## How the client arrives (Money 03)

The right client is a **curious / crack** person with an innovative project or a technology, who needs someone on the design and creative side.

They **approach**. They already trust. They understand the process enough that the work gets easier — not a committee of “why is it like this” from people who do not get it.

**Doctor-trust:** like a doctor — you do not walk in to judge; you hear what you need; you can still have a better conversation. kiruk still **makes the best out of the client**, then does more than the brief. The money follows that feeling, not a flagship first month.

This is the _relationship_ of one-to-one, not a price list.

## Who sits in the room (People 06)

Not the market-follower who wants the basic version of what everyone else shipped.

The one: passion, a vision they will take to the extreme, an idea they cannot yet execute or say. They hire for strategy + idea + creative path — brand, site/MVP/app, process, creative direction — so the thing becomes a **whole** they can sustain. They want to be seen, ready to launch, professionally set, in the same vibe.

**Face:** Sanxia — illustrator, 3D gallery, tech frustration, limited time. Full snapshot: [people.md](./people.md).

## What this is not

- Not a personal freelance site with no studio name (still kiruk, not a personal URL).
- Not many juniors executing a deck.
- Not “sustain the brand as a company” as the point of this doc.
