# Code of Conduct — Kiruk

> Kiruk is a studio and a movement (kirukism). Our work is open, collaborative, and weird. This Code of Conduct exists so people across cultures, skill levels, and disciplines can show up and contribute without being made to feel small.

This Code adapts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

---

## Our pledge

We — kirukan (studio co-workers), kirukargals (collaborators), contributors, and learners — pledge to make participation in kiruk a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Kirukism standards

Aligned with the studio's principles:

**Encouraged:**
- Sharing rough scribbles and dead ends openly — the kirukal layer is the work.
- Honest critique focused on the artifact, not the person.
- Citing the source when you build on someone's idea.
- Asking obvious questions; assuming nothing.
- Naming the work in the studio's vocabulary (universe, ISM, kirukargal, scribble) once you've read the docs.

**Not tolerated:**
- Personal attacks, sexualized language, trolling, doxxing.
- Dismissing someone's idea because of their background, accent, or unfamiliarity with the stack.
- Reusing kiruk content without attribution (see [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE) — content is CC-BY 4.0).
- Posting client-private material in public channels.
- Demanding free labor from contributors or kirukargals.

---

## Scope

This Code applies in all kiruk spaces: this repo (issues, PRs, discussions), `kiruk.studio`, ISM project deploys, social channels (`@kirukism`), and any kirukargal collaboration. It also applies when a contributor is officially representing kiruk in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the founder privately:

- Email: `praveenmanikandan2000@gmail.com` (subject line: `[kiruk CoC]`).
- All reports are kept confidential. The founder will review within 7 days.

Do **not** disclose specifics of a CoC issue in public issues, PRs, or social posts.

---

## Enforcement

The founder is the current enforcement maintainer. As the kirukargal network grows, a small council will share this role (see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](./GOVERNANCE.md)).

Consequences scale with severity:

1. **Correction** — private clarification of what behavior was inappropriate and why.
2. **Warning** — written warning + temporary block from a specific surface (issue, PR thread).
3. **Temporary ban** — N-day block from all kiruk spaces.
4. **Permanent ban** — full removal from kirukargal access; case logged privately.
