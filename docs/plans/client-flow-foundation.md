### Batch 1 — arrival

1. When you say “we got a client,” what just happened — a paid yes, a chemistry call booked, a DM, or they started `/onboard`?
2. Who is the client for kiruk: founder with a weird product, brand team, kirukargal, or all of those?
3. Who is _not_ a client, even if they can pay?
4. Should one person be the only client contact, or can a committee arrive?
5. Does every universe start with a human call, or can they arrive fully async via `/onboard`?
6. What must exist before you will spend a second hour on them — scribble, one-sentence idea, both, or something else?
7. Is `/onboard` the client’s arrival, or is that name wrong and it should be “start a universe / discovery”?
8. `/call` vs `/onboard` vs you creating a universe on the dashboard — which is the front door, and which are side doors?
9. After arrival, who owns the next move — them, you, or both with a named next step?
10. What does “they went off track with the idea” look like in one real example from your head?

### Batch 2 — chemistry, lock, qualify

11. On the first call, are we judging fit, extracting the idea, or selling kiruk?
12. What three questions would you actually ask on that call — not a 20-question form?
13. The lock: is it one sentence, a scribble, or sentence + scribble?
14. Who writes the lock — they, you restating them, or you both until it matches?
15. If their words and “what we heard” disagree, who wins?
16. Qualify: keep only kiruk non-negotiables (trend / boring / shallow), or also ASAP / no decision-maker / hostile money-talk?
17. Disqualify: a polite no and stop, or a no plus a referral?
18. Do we ever charge for discovery, or is Face 1 always free?
19. How long may a lead sit without a lock before you close it?
20. Grid: a qualify tool, a visual deliverable, or drop the word?

### Batch 3 — proposal, money, contract, portal
